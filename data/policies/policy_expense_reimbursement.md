# Expense Reimbursement & Travel Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses the Company will pay for, which expenses require advance approval, what documentation employees must retain, and how reimbursement works.

The goal is to make business travel and reasonable business spending easy while preventing accidental or intentional misuse of Company funds.

## 2. Who is covered

The policy applies to employees who incur approved business expenses. Contractors and consultants follow their contracts unless Finance confirms otherwise.

Business expenses must have a legitimate business purpose. Personal expenses should never be submitted as business expenses.

## 3. Approval tiers

- **Under $500 equivalent:** direct manager approval.
- **$500–$5,000 equivalent:** manager approval plus department-head approval.
- **Over $5,000 equivalent:** manager, department-head, and VP approval **before** the expense is incurred.

For India employees, Finance may publish INR limits or local travel matrices that replace the equivalent-dollar thresholds.

## 4. Flights and transportation

Economy class is the standard for domestic and short-haul travel. Premium or business class may be approved for longer international journeys where the travel matrix permits it.

Reasonable taxi, rideshare, metro, train, airport-transfer, toll, and parking costs for approved business travel are reimbursable.

Employees should select reasonably priced transportation consistent with safety, travel time, and business requirements.

## 5. Hotels and accommodation

Employees should use Company-negotiated hotels where available. If no preferred hotel exists, a reasonable business hotel is reimbursable.

Room upgrades, minibar purchases, movies, spa services, personal laundry unrelated to an extended business trip, and other personal charges are not normally reimbursable.

## 6. Meals

Reasonable meals during approved business travel are reimbursable. Client meals must identify the business purpose and attendees.

Alcohol at client meals above $75 per person requires VP approval under the global policy. In India, employees must also follow local law, Company conduct standards, and venue restrictions.

Personal meals during normal work from the employee's regular location are not reimbursable unless specifically covered by an approved event or travel arrangement.

## 7. Home-office expenses

Remote-eligible employees may receive a one-time home-office stipend of $500 equivalent within the first 90 days of a new fully remote or hybrid arrangement, subject to the applicable local reimbursement process.

Typical eligible items include an approved monitor, keyboard, mouse, desk accessories, or ergonomic equipment. Personal entertainment equipment is not covered.

## 8. Receipts and missing receipts

Employees should attach itemized receipts and provide:
- date;
- amount and currency;
- vendor;
- business purpose;
- attendees where relevant;
- project/client, if required;
- manager or pre-approval reference.

If a receipt is genuinely unavailable, the employee should provide a written explanation. Finance may approve or reject the claim.

## 9. Submission deadlines

Submit expenses through the HR/Finance portal within **30 days** of the expense.

Claims submitted after 60 days will normally not be reimbursed unless Finance accepts documented extenuating circumstances, such as hospitalization, extended business travel, system failure, or another reasonable cause.

## 10. Corporate cards

Employees issued a corporate card should use it for eligible business expenses rather than paying personally.

Corporate-card transactions remain subject to receipt and business-purpose requirements. A corporate card is not permission to spend outside policy.

## 11. India-specific travel

For domestic travel within India, employees should use the Company's published travel matrix for flight/train class, hotel limits, and local transportation.

Travel involving multiple cities should be planned to minimize unnecessary cost while meeting business needs.

International travel from India may require passport, visa, immigration, tax, insurance, and other documentation. Employees must obtain required Company approvals before booking.

## 12. Cancellations and unused bookings

If a trip is cancelled, employees must promptly cancel refundable bookings and return unused tickets or credits where possible.

Cancellation fees caused by business changes are normally reimbursable. Personal cancellation costs may not be.

## 13. Fraud and misuse

Submitting altered receipts, duplicate claims, personal expenses as business expenses, or deliberately false information may result in repayment, loss of expense privileges, and disciplinary action.

## 14. Reimbursement timing

Approved employee-paid expenses are normally paid by direct deposit in the next standard payroll/payment cycle after final approval. Actual timing may vary because of banking, payroll, or month-end processing.
