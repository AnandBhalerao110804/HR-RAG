# Remote Work & Flexible Hours Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains when employees can work remotely, how hybrid schedules operate, expected availability, equipment, security, travel, and cross-border work.

Remote work is intended to provide flexibility without reducing accountability, collaboration, customer support, information security, or delivery standards.

## 2. Eligibility

Eligibility is primarily determined by role and business requirements.

Roles involving physical equipment, in-person customer service, facilities, warehouses, retail, laboratories, or other location-dependent work may not be remote-eligible.

For eligible roles, the default arrangement may be remote unless the manager documents a legitimate business reason for another arrangement.

## 3. Work arrangements

Eligible employees may have:

- **Fully remote:** no routine office attendance requirement.
- **Hybrid:** minimum two days per week on-site, normally coordinated with the team.
- **Fully on-site:** required by role or selected by the employee where available.

The employment letter, HR portal, or manager-confirmed work arrangement is the authoritative record.

## 4. Core hours

All employees should be reachable between **10:00 AM and 3:00 PM local time** unless their role, shift, approved accommodation, or team arrangement states otherwise.

Being “reachable” does not mean sitting in meetings continuously. Employees are expected to maintain reasonable response times and attend meetings relevant to their responsibilities.

## 5. Working from another city in India

Employees may request temporary work from another Indian city, such as working from a family home during a personal visit.

For short periods, manager approval may be sufficient. Longer arrangements may require People Operations review because of payroll, tax, establishment, security, attendance, or business-continuity considerations.

Employees should not assume that working from another city permanently is automatically allowed.

## 6. Working from another country

Working remotely from a country other than the employee's employing entity's country requires advance approval from People Operations and Legal.

Approval is required because immigration, tax, employment-law, permanent-establishment, data-protection, and insurance implications may arise.

A vacation abroad does not automatically create permission to work from that country.

## 7. Office attendance and team days

Hybrid employees should attend agreed team days unless their manager approves an exception.

Occasional remote days may be approved for practical reasons such as home repairs, family logistics, medical appointments, severe weather, or temporary travel, subject to operational requirements.

## 8. Equipment

The Company provides a laptop and standard peripherals to eligible employees.

Employees must protect Company equipment from theft, damage, unauthorized access, and unsafe use. Lost or stolen devices should be reported to IT immediately.

## 9. Home-office stipend

A one-time home-office stipend of $500 equivalent is available within the first 90 days of starting a fully remote or hybrid arrangement, subject to the Expense Reimbursement & Travel Policy and local currency limits.

## 10. Security and confidentiality

Remote employees must:
- use Company-approved accounts and devices;
- avoid sharing credentials;
- use secure networks;
- lock screens when away;
- avoid discussing confidential information in public places;
- follow Company security and data-handling procedures.

Public Wi-Fi should not be used for sensitive work unless the Company's approved security controls are active.

## 11. Performance and attendance

Remote work does not change performance expectations.

Employees are expected to be available during agreed working hours, attend required meetings, meet deadlines, communicate blockers, and maintain accurate leave/attendance records.

Working remotely while on approved leave is not expected. If an employee works during leave, they should discuss the situation with their manager and People Operations so the leave record can be corrected where appropriate.

## 12. Internet and utilities

Normal household internet and utility costs are generally the employee's responsibility unless a local addendum or employment arrangement states otherwise.

The Company may reimburse approved connectivity or equipment costs where specifically listed in the applicable expense policy.

## 13. Health, safety, and ergonomics

Employees should maintain a reasonably safe workspace and use appropriate ergonomic practices.

Work-related injuries or safety concerns should be reported through the applicable process. Employees should not continue working in conditions that present an immediate safety risk.

## 14. Flexible hours

Managers may approve adjusted schedules for legitimate personal circumstances, provided business coverage and collaboration are maintained.

Flexible hours do not automatically reduce the employee's contractual working time.

## 15. Practical examples

**Example 1:** An employee normally works hybrid and needs to work from home for two days because their apartment has a plumbing repair. They inform the manager and remain available during core hours.

**Example 2:** An employee wants to spend three weeks in another country while continuing to work. They must obtain advance People Operations and Legal approval before making the arrangement.

**Example 3:** A remote employee has a doctor's appointment at 11 AM. They block the calendar, inform relevant colleagues, and use the appropriate leave/flexible-hours process rather than disappearing during core hours.
