# Paid Time Off (PTO) & Vacation Policy

## 1. Purpose

Paid Time Off allows employees to take planned breaks from work for holidays, travel, rest, personal commitments, and other purposes.

PTO should be used responsibly. Employees are encouraged to take regular breaks rather than allowing workload to prevent them from using reasonable leave.

## 2. Annual accrual

PTO accrues monthly based on continuous service:

- **0–2 years:** 15 days/year (1.25 days/month)
- **2–5 years:** 20 days/year (1.67 days/month)
- **5+ years:** 25 days/year (2.08 days/month)

Accrual begins on the employee's official start date and is prorated for mid-month joiners.

## 3. India leave structure

For India employees, People Operations may maintain separate state/establishment-specific leave categories such as earned/privilege leave, casual leave, sick leave, public holidays, and other statutory leave.

The applicable India or state-specific addendum takes precedence where local law provides a greater entitlement or a different accrual/carry-forward rule.

Employees should therefore use the leave category shown in the HR portal rather than manually converting every absence into PTO.

## 4. Planned leave requests

Employees should normally submit planned PTO at least **5 business days in advance**.

For one or two days, manager approval may be sufficient. Requests of 4 or more consecutive days should also be visible to the department head for workforce planning.

Long vacations should be discussed with the manager early, particularly during product launches, audits, customer commitments, or other known peak periods.

## 5. Emergency or same-day leave

Not every absence can be planned.

If an employee wakes up sick, has a family emergency, or faces another unexpected situation, they should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

The employee can regularize the absence in the HR system when they are able to do so. A same-day emergency should not be treated as a failure to provide five business days' notice.

## 6. Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from planned vacation where provided by the applicable India/state leave rules or Company addendum.

For extended illness, People Operations may request appropriate documentation where permitted by law and reasonably necessary for administration. Employees should not disclose detailed medical information to their manager unless necessary.

## 7. Half-days and hourly absences

The HR system may permit half-day or hourly leave where the relevant leave category supports it.

Employees should not be required to take a full vacation day for a short approved appointment if the applicable leave category permits a smaller unit.

## 8. Carry-forward

Under the global baseline, up to 5 unused PTO days may carry into the following calendar year.

For India employees, carry-forward and lapse rules will follow the applicable state/establishment leave rules where those rules are more generous or mandatory.

The HR portal should be treated as the source of the employee's current available balance.

## 9. Leave during notice period

Employees may request PTO during a notice period, but approval depends on business requirements, the employment contract, and applicable law.

Unused accrued leave may be settled according to the Company's separation process and applicable Indian law.

## 10. Holidays and weekends

Company holidays, weekly rest days, and approved non-working days are not normally deducted from PTO.

If an employee voluntarily works on a holiday or weekly rest day, any compensatory-off or additional payment entitlement will be handled under the applicable local policy and law.

## 11. Combining leave types

PTO is separate from parental leave, medical leave, bereavement leave, and other protected leave.

Employees should not be required to consume PTO before receiving a separate statutory or Company leave entitlement.

Where two leave categories could apply to the same period, People Operations determines the appropriate coding.

## 12. Manager approval and business continuity

Managers should approve reasonable leave requests whenever operationally possible.

Managers should not create informal rules such as “no leave during the whole quarter” or indefinitely prevent employees from using accrued leave.

If multiple employees request the same dates, the manager may use business coverage, fairness, and request timing to determine sequencing.

## 13. Leave cancellation

Employees may cancel or amend an approved request if plans change. The employee should update the HR system promptly so the balance and attendance record remain accurate.

Managers may ask an employee to reconsider dates only where there is a genuine business continuity concern.

## 14. Separation and payout

Unused accrued PTO will be settled according to applicable law, the employee's leave category, and the Company's separation process.

Because Indian leave encashment rules can differ by establishment and leave type, Payroll/People Operations will determine the applicable treatment rather than applying the global five-day carry-forward rule mechanically.

## 15. Practical examples

**Example 1:** An employee books a family vacation for seven working days. They submit the request three weeks in advance, and the manager checks project coverage before approving it.

**Example 2:** An employee develops a fever on Monday morning and cannot work. They message their manager and later record the appropriate sick leave category. They are not expected to have requested it five business days earlier.

**Example 3:** Two employees request the same week off. The manager reviews deadlines and coverage and discusses alternatives fairly rather than automatically rejecting both requests.
